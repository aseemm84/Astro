The casting ceremony

The nine grahas winding back to a moment, and settling onto the nakṣatra ring where they stood. Turn it, tilt it, and run it for any moment you like.

Everywhere else on this site the ceremony is a veil: it covers the second or two while a chart is computed, and lifts when the chart is ready. That makes it something you see and cannot look at. This page is the same ceremony with nothing behind it — no chart waiting, no clock running out — so it can be watched, turned and run again.

What you are looking atदृश्य-विवरण

The ring is the nakṣatra ring: twenty-seven divisions of 13°20′ each, running anticlockwise from the start of Aśvinī. The nine discs are the grahas, drawn from the same images the Motion scenes use. They begin where the sky stands now and wind back to where it stood at the moment in the field above, trailing the path they took to get there.

The wind-back is the point. A chart is not a picture of the sky as it is; it is a picture of the sky as it was, at one place, at one instant. The ceremony is that sentence drawn rather than written.

Study note. The positions are the engine's own — the same sidereal longitudes the chart report uses, under whichever ayanāṃśa this browser is set to. The ring is to scale and the discs are not: a graha drawn at its true angular size would be invisible. Nothing here is a chart, and nothing here is read for meaning; it is the arithmetic of a chart, moving.

Turning itपरिभ्रमण

  • Drag the ring to spin it. It keeps the spin when you let go.
  • Reveal, Escape or a click on the backdrop ends it.
  • It also ends itself after a while, so a window left open does not keep animating.
  • If this machine is set to reduced motion, the drift stops and the wind-back is brief. That is the system preference being honoured, not a fault.

Accuracy & limitsसीमा

The longitudes are computed here, by the engine this site carries, and nothing is fetched to draw them. The ring is a teaching diagram: it shows where the grahas were in longitude, and says nothing about latitude, distance or visibility. A graha shown at the top of the ring was not overhead.

Handle with care. This is an illustration of a calculation. It is not a prediction, and nothing about a person can be read from it.
