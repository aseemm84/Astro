Here & now asks the browser where this device is and casts a chart for that place at the present moment — useful for a praśna question, or simply to see the sky as it stands. The position comes from the browser, the clock and the UTC offset from the device, and the nearest town from the city list already on this page; nothing is sent anywhere. It needs the site to be open over https, so it will not work from a file opened directly off disk.

Fifteen of the animations are about the sky rather than about you: the eclipses, the tilt of the Moon’s path, the pañcā–aṅga, the phases. The nine that read your chart stay behind the form above, and each says why.

Vārṣika Gochara — a year of transits


This needs no birth chart. Gochara is where the nine grahas
actually are, so a year of it is a fact about the sky rather than about any
person. Pick a year and every rāśi ingress, every retrograde station and
every return to direct motion is computed from the same ephemeris that
casts the charts above — not copied from a table, so it follows whichever
ayanāṃśa you have selected.



  
  
  
  






Twelve Candra-rāśi readings


The same year read from each janma-rāśi in turn: which bhāva each graha
occupies counted from that rāśi, for how long, and which of the classical
gochara conditions are running. Every rule says which tier it comes from —
a BPHS verse, a note by the translator of this edition, or later tradition
— because transit doctrine is where those three are most often blurred
together.
