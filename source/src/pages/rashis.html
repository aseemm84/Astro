The zodiac is twelve rashis of 30° each, measured sidereally from 0° Aries. A sign lends its lord, element, modality, gender and direction to any planet or house that falls in it — so sign qualities quietly drive dignity, aspects, vargas and dashas alike.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 4 (descriptions and classifications of the signs; the Kalapurusha limb-scheme).

The working classifications

Chara / Sthira / Dvisvabhava (movable / fixed / dual) — the modality. Movable signs initiate and change; fixed signs consolidate and resist; dual (common) signs adapt and mediate. Modality alone decides Rashi Drishti (sign aspects).

Tattva (element) — fire = drive & spirit; earth = substance & practicality; air = intellect & exchange; water = feeling & flow. Same-element signs are naturally sympathetic.

Oja / Yugma (odd / even) — odd signs are male, even signs female. Parity drives Ojhayugmarasi Bala and the Hora and Navamsa reckonings, and matters for the strength of male vs female planets.

Shirshodaya / Prishtodaya / Ubhayodaya — signs that rise head-first, back-first, or both. Head-rising signs are considered stronger by day; this feeds day/night strength and muhurta.

Fruitful / barren, and other sub-classes — water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) are 'fruitful' and favour fertility questions; Aries, Leo and the first half of Sagittarius are quadruped; Gemini, Virgo, Libra, first half of Sagittarius and Aquarius are human; the rest are watery/insect — used in fine delineation of appearance, conduct and progeny.

Kalapurusha (the cosmic body) — the signs map onto a body from Aries (head) to Pisces (feet) — used to locate marks, injuries and health matters by sign and house.

Sign lords and dual rulership

The Sun and Moon each rule a single sign (Leo, Cancer). The other five planets rule two signs — one 'day/male' and one 'night/female' — and generally give fuller strength in the sign matching their own gender (e.g. Mars is stronger in fiery Aries than in watery Scorpio; Venus in earthy Taurus more than airy Libra).

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 3 (own-sign strength: male planets in male signs and female planets in female signs gain full strength; the note on Mars in Aries vs Scorpio and Venus in Taurus vs Libra).

Study note. Don't read a sign as a personality alone. In practice a sign matters as (a) the lord it hands a house to, (b) the dignity it confers on any planet sitting there, and (c) the modality that sets its sign-aspects. Those three are what actually move a reading.
