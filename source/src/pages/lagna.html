The Lagna (ascendant) is the degree of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of birth. It anchors the whole chart: every house is counted from it, and it signifies the body, the self, and the overall shape of life.

Lagna — ascendant — the rising sign & degree. Its lord (Lagnesha) and any planet in or aspecting it are primary to vitality, temperament, and how life unfolds.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 3–4 (the rising sign as Lagna; roughly two hours per sign, varying by latitude — about 1° every four minutes of clock time).

Study note. The single most useful step in any chart: identify the Lagna lord and see where it sits, how dignified it is, and what aspects it. A strong, well-placed Lagnesha is a foundation the rest of the chart builds on.

Special ascendants

Beyond the rasi Lagna, BPHS uses several computed ascendants as alternative reference points, each advancing from sunrise at its own rate.

Special lagnaAdvances one sign per…Chiefly used for
Bhava Lagna5 ghatis (2 hours) from sunrisea secondary self / body reference in yogas & strength
Hora Lagna2½ ghatis (1 hour) from sunrisewealth and financial standing
Ghatika (Ghati) Lagna1 ghati (24 min) from sunrisepower, status and authority
Arudha Lagna (Pada)(computed from the Lagna lord — see Special Points)public image, how the self is perceived
Sree Lagna(from the Moon's nakshatra position and the ascendant)prosperity and the goddess of fortune
Pranapada(from the Sun's longitude in ghatis)vitality and birth-time rectification

Source BPHS, Vol. 1 — the special-lagna material; Bhava / Hora / Ghatika Lagna are invoked together in the Raja Yoga chapter (Ch. 39, v. 24), and Arudha Lagna is computed in Ch. 29.

Translator / commentary. Editions differ in how many special ascendants they detail and in the exact ghati rates. Verify the figures against the PDF before using them in calculation.
Handle with care. All special ascendants are time-sensitive — only as reliable as the recorded birth time. They are refinements layered on the rasi Lagna, never replacements for it. When the birth time is uncertain, rectify (using Pranapada, major life events, and the Navamsa lagna) before leaning on them.
