Dignity tells you a placement's quality; bala (strength) tells you how much of it can actually manifest. BPHS scores strength in Virupas (Shashtiamsas) — 60 Virupas make 1 Rupa. Only the seven planets from the Sun to Saturn are scored; the nodes are not.

Shadbala — the sixfold strength

Six sources of strength are computed for each planet and summed. Each also has a required minimum; a planet meeting or exceeding its requirement is 'strong' and reliably delivers its promise.

1 · Sthana Bala — positional strength

The largest and most-used component, built from five sub-parts:

  • Uchcha Bala (exaltation): distance from the debilitation point ÷ 3, max 60 Virupas — full at exaltation, zero at debilitation.
  • Saptavargaja Bala: dignity summed across seven vargas (Rasi, Hora, Drekkana, Saptamsa, Navamsa, Dvadasamsa, Trimsamsa), by the fivefold relationship —
  • Ojhayugmarasi-amsa Bala: strength by placement in odd/even signs and navamsas (male planets favour odd, female even).
  • Kendradi Bala: 60 Virupas in a kendra, 30 in a panapara, 15 in an apoklima.
  • Drekkana Bala: 15 Virupas for the appropriate planet-sex in the 1st/2nd/3rd drekkana.

Each planet is strongest in a particular angle (max 60 Virupas), weakening to zero in the opposite angle:

3 · Kala Bala — temporal strength

  • Nathonnatha (day/night): benefics gain by day, malefics by night (Mercury always).
  • Paksha Bala: benefics strong in the bright fortnight, malefics in the dark.
  • Tribhaga Bala: strength by the third of day/night ruled by the planet.
  • Varsha / Masa / Dina / Hora lords: the lords of the year, month, weekday and hour gain strength.
  • Ayana Bala: strength by declension (north/south of the equator).
  • Yuddha Bala: the outcome of a planetary war adjusts the combatants.

4 · Cheshta Bala — motional strength

Strength from motion: a retrograde planet scores near-maximum; a combust or fast-accelerating planet scores low. (The Sun and Moon take Ayana and Paksha respectively in its place.)

Bhava Bala — the strength of a house

Houses are scored too, from: the strength of the house-lord (Bhavadhipati Bala), the house's directional strength (Bhava Dig Bala), and the net aspects on the house (Bhava Drik Bala). A strong house delivers its significations; a weak one under-delivers even under a capable lord.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 27 (Bhava Bala components).

Ishta and Kashta Bala — benefic vs difficult potency

From a planet's exaltation-ray (Uchcha Rasmi) and motion-ray (Cheshta Rasmi), BPHS derives two opposed potencies that predict the flavour of its results:

Ishta Phala — benefic potency — the capacity to give agreeable results. Roughly derived from the mean of the two rays; a high-Ishta planet blesses the matters it touches.

Kashta Phala — difficult potency — its complement (Ishta and Kashta relate to a fixed total). A high-Kashta planet stresses the matters it touches.

A parallel Subhanka (benefic-point) scale grades a planet by dignity: 60 exalted, 45 Moolatrikona, 30 own, 22 great-friend, 15 friend, 8 neutral, 4 enemy, 2 great-enemy, 0 debilitated (halved in other vargas). Directional and temporal strengths likewise translate into good vs difficult tendencies.

Source BPHS, Vol. 1, Ch. 28 (Ishta & Kashta phala from the Uchcha and Cheshta rays; the Subhanka scale).

Study note. Full Shadbala needs exact longitudes and software. Without them, reason qualitatively and you'll capture most of it: is the planet dignified (Uchcha/Saptavargaja), angular (Dig/Kendradi), in helpful motion (retrograde vs combust — Cheshta), and benefically aspected (Drik)? Those four questions echo four of the six components.
