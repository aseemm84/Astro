If you are offline

This page can also appear when you are offline and ask for a page your browser has not stored. Everything on this site is computed in your own browser and nothing is sent anywhere, so the pages you have already opened will keep working without a connection — but a page you have never opened has nothing to work from. Reconnect, open it once, and it will be there the next time you are offline.
