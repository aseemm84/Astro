Ask for a reelरील माँगिए

Say what the reel should be about, in Hindi, Hinglish or English — “रोहिणी नक्षत्र पर 45 सेकंड की रील”, “Diwali ki tareekh kyon badalti hai”, “Shani vakri explained”. The page chooses the layout, writes the narration from the engine’s own numbers, voices it and renders a vertical video you can preview, edit and save.

How it decidesकैसे चुनता है

Offline (the default). The request is matched on this device against the layouts’ names — every nakṣatra and rāśi by its Hindi or English name, the festivals, eclipses, retrogrades and the rest — and the engine’s own script is used. No key, no network.

Connected (only if you switch it on, with your own Claude or Gemini key). An agent reads the request, picks the layout, reads that layout’s computed facts and writes a short Hindi story over them. Before anything is sent the panel shows exactly what will go, and to which service. Every number in its script is checked against the facts and the script is refused until it passes. Your birth details and kundli are never sent.

Your reelsआपकी रीलें

Every reel you make is kept in this browser (or in the desktop app) — never on a server. Open one to watch it again, re-edit its script or save the file again.

Nothing is posted anywhere. Publishing a reel is always something you do yourself.
