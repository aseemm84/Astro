Method & provenancewhat is computed, what is asserted

Positions, dignities, vargas, ashtakavarga, yogas, arudhas, karakas, avasthās and the daśā tree come from the bharatephemeris engine (bharat-ephemeris-web · v∞.4 · MK-Ayanāṃśa · sealed 2026-05-11). Houses from the lagna, nakṣatra-pādas, combustion, Moolatrikona by degree, the full mahādaśā sequence and the Jupiter antardaśā schedule are recomputed independently here and agree with the engine wherever both produce a value.

Known limitsThe ayanāṃśa is ≈ Lahiri (the engine's own stamp reads “MK-Ayanāṃśa”), not certified Lahiri. Several planets sit at the very end of their signs — Sun 29.9° Scorpio, Saturn 29.4° Libra, Mars 28.8° Capricorn, Mercury 28.2° Scorpio — which makes the avasthā layer, D60 and exact sub-period dates sensitive to the recorded birth minute. The engine treats Ketu as owning Scorpio, a disputed convention. Avasthā percentages and yoga verdicts are its computations, not verses.
