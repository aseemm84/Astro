What is here, and what is notक्षेत्र

Every scene on this page is about the sky as it stands, so it runs with no birth data entered at all. The place comes from this machine, and the moment is now.

Study note. Nine of the forty-one scenes are unavailable here, and the panel says so on each rather than hiding them. Three are irreducibly natal: birth sky is the sky at one person's birth moment, daśā runs from the Moon's nakṣatra at birth, and Sade Sati measures Śani's passage against that same Moon. Gochara draws the natal chart inside the transiting sky, and frames is anchored to the birth moment and place. The remaining four — lagna, bhāva, rāśi and the full nakṣatra strip — use the chart only to place your grahas on a teaching diagram, and could be freed by substituting here-and-now for birth. That is a later pass, and it needs the frame to say plainly that a chart cast for now is a praśna chart and not your own.

The scenes draw from the same engine as everything else on this site, so a figure shown here and the same figure in a chart report come from one computation rather than two.
