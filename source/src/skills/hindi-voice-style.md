# Hindi voice style

How every Reel Agent narration sounds. The other skills include this one.

## Language
- Default language is **pure Hindi in Devanagari** (शुद्ध हिंदी). Use तत्सम words a
  Jyotiṣa listener expects (नक्षत्र, राशि, ग्रह, अंश, भाव, तिथि), but keep sentences
  conversational, the way a good teacher speaks — not a textbook.
- English only for proper names the facts give in English (a star such as Aldebaran,
  a catalogue such as HYG). Never translate a star's catalogue name.
- Hinglish, when chosen: Roman script, Hindi grammar, Sanskrit terms in their usual
  spoken form (nakshatra, rashi, graha).

## Numbers
- Write numbers in **digits** in the narration (40°, 13°20′, 2026). The voice layer
  turns them into spoken Hindi; digits are what the fact check reads most reliably.
- Every number must come from `get_facts`. If a number would help and is not in the
  facts, leave it out — do not round, estimate or recall one.

## Shape of a reel
- **5 to 9 lines**, one per beat range, together **35–60 seconds** of speech.
- Line 1 is the **hook**: a question or a surprising true fact from the facts, in under
  ten words.
- The last line is the **call to action**: an invitation to check it or to comment
  ("Stellarium में स्वयं जाँच लीजिए।", "आपका जन्म नक्षत्र कौन सा है? नीचे लिखिए।").
  Never "follow for predictions".
- A line should be speakable in one breath: 8–20 words.

## Captions
- At most **4 words**, Devanagari, carrying the line's key fact ("49 तारे",
  "8 नवंबर 2026", "40°00′ से 53°20′").

## Never
- No predictions about any person, no remedies, no fear. Where the topic touches a
  chart or a planet's "effect", say "शास्त्रीय अध्ययन का ढाँचा, भविष्यवाणी नहीं।"
- Mythology is **story**, said as story ("कथा है कि…"), never as fact.
- No claims about health, money, marriage, children or death.
