What a failure would mean. These are not cosmetic checks. They assert things that must be true of any correct implementation — that Sarvāṣṭakavarga totals 337, that Ṣaḍbala equals the sum of its six components, that Yoginī’s eight terms total 36 years, that houses follow from longitudes. A red row means the engine is wrong, not that the test is strict.
On the Parashara’s Light comparison. That software is an independent implementation, not an oracle. Where the two agree, both are probably right. Where they differ, the cause is usually a stated convention — the sunrise definition, or the ārūḍha exception rule — and those differences are documented on the pages themselves rather than quietly reconciled.
